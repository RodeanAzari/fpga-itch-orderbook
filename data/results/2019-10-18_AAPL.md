## Interpretation

The unbounded model processed all 1,669,591 AAPL messages (of 302,347,067 in the day) with
zero unknown refs, duplicates, over-reductions or malformed messages, so the parser and book
are self-consistent on real data. Peak concurrent live orders was 27,814 and the largest
order ref 287,271,257 (29 bits).

The window base is the mid of the first two-sided, uncrossed BBO at or after 09:30:
23,455 ticks ($234.55). A ±2,048-tick window contains 831,837 of 831,839 two-sided BBOs. The
two exceptions are thin-market moments at the session edges: 04:00:11 (bid $175.12, ask
$236.90) and 20:00:00 (bid $234.00, ask $260.00). 16,099 of 870,964 adds (1.85 %) priced out
of window, including 9 sub-penny stub quotes, so `price_oow` will be non-zero as expected.

`_table_fill.csv` shows that a single-hash 4-way table overflows at every feasible size, even
with a good hash, because worst-case bucket load follows Poisson statistics at the peak.
`_table_dleft.csv` shows the two-bank d-left table (2 × 8,192 sets × 4 ways) had zero
overflow all day. That is one day of evidence; see ADR 0001.

`_bounded.csv` runs the golden model in the accepted RTL configuration (d-left 2 × 8,192 × 4,
base 23,455, ±2,048 ticks) beside the unbounded model.
- `table_overflow`, `ref_overflow`, `shares_overflow` and `unknown_ref` are all 0.
- `price_subpenny`, `duplicate_ref` and `over_reduce` are 0, so `book_stale` (ADR 0006: any
  silent divergence) stayed 0 for the whole day.
- `price_oow` is 16,099, matching `adds_out_of_window` from the independent stats run.
- `bbo_oow` is 4 flagged updates across the two out-of-window episodes.
- On every one of the 1,669,591 messages, the out-of-window flag was set exactly when the
  bounded BBO differed from the true one (0 violations).

The per-minute plot samples the last BBO in each minute, so it does not show the two
out-of-window moments.
